# 💙🦉 CiviQuest — Civil Service Exam Reviewer

**Practice turns into a simple app.**

CiviQuest is an Android-based Civil Service Examination Reviewer designed to help aspiring government employees prepare for the **Professional and Subprofessional Civil Service Examinations** through interactive quizzes, practice questions, explanations, and mock examinations.

Study smarter, improve your knowledge, track your progress, and prepare for your next examination with CiviQuest!

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Comprehensive Review Categories

Practice different subjects covered by the Civil Service Examination:

- Verbal Ability
- Numerical Ability
- General Information
- Analytical Ability
- Clerical Ability

### 🎯 Interactive Quiz System

- Professional and Subprofessional examination levels
- Randomized questions and answers
- Multiple-choice questions
- Explanations for answers
- Configurable quiz sessions and difficulty levels

### 📝 Mock Examination

Experience a practice examination environment and evaluate your readiness for the actual Civil Service Examination.

### 🏆 Gamification and Achievements

- Earn points and XP.
- Unlock achievements and badges.
- Collect and customize owl avatars.
- Track your personal progress.

### 📊 Study Progress Tracking

- Monitor quiz accuracy.
- Track study time.
- Review personal best scores.
- Bookmark important questions.
- Review previously incorrect answers.

### 🦉 Personalized Owl Experience

Enjoy a friendly owl-themed interface with collectible avatars and customizable themes.

## 🎓 Who Can Use CiviQuest?

CiviQuest is intended for:

- Students preparing for Civil Service Examinations.
- Graduates planning to apply for government positions.
